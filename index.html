02

What the app does

Track

Renewals, not surprises

Monthly, quarterly and yearly cycles, trials that end, prices that change, and a calendar that shows the month you are actually about to pay for.

Convert

One currency

A subscription billed in dollars and one billed in euros add up correctly, using reference rates published by the ECB and other central banks.

Keep

Yours to take away

Export everything to a JSON file whenever you want. Sign in only if you want a copy you can restore on another phone.

What it does not do

No bank connection. Ever.

SubDesk does not link to a bank account, does not read transactions, and has no field anywhere in it for a card number, an IBAN or an account number. It knows what you tell it and nothing else.
