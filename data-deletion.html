01

From inside the app

If you signed in

Delete the account

Open Settings → Account → Delete account and confirm. This deletes your sign-in credentials and the copy of your records stored in your account area, in one operation. You may be asked to sign in again first — that is the identity check, not an extra step we could skip.

If you never signed in

Uninstall

Nothing about you exists outside your phone, so removing the app removes everything. Android deletes the app's database along with the app.

Export first if you want to keep a copy. Settings → Export data writes every subscription, payment and income entry to a JSON file you choose the location of. Deletion cannot be undone.

02

What is deleted, and when

DataDeletedTiming
Subscriptions, payments, income, settings held in your accountYesImmediately
Email address and sign-in credentialsYesImmediately
The database on your phoneYesWhen you uninstall or clear app storage
Server backups held by our hosting providerYesWithin 30 days, as those backups rotate out

SubDesk keeps no separate analytics store, no marketing list and no shadow copy of your records. There is nothing left over once the two rows above are done.
